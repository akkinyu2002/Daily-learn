About Us

A cafe for everyone

We started Chai & Co. because we wanted a place in Hyderabad where you could get proper Irani chai and a good espresso under the same roof. Add some shawarma, burgers, momos, and homemade desserts — and that's us.

We're in Banjara Hills. The vibe is casual. Prices are fair. Everything is made fresh. Whether you're grabbing a quick chai or sitting down for a long lunch, you're welcome.
